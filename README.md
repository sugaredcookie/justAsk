# JustAsk

> A student-to-student community platform where skills, opportunities, and collaboration meet.

JustAsk is a platform designed to help students connect with one another beyond academics. Whether you want to learn a new skill, earn some money, find help with a task, or simply volunteer your time, JustAsk provides a trusted community where students can help students.

The platform revolves around three primary categories:

- 🎓 Skill Exchange
- 💼 Work & Earn
- 🤝 Volunteer

In addition, JustAsk introduces seasonal community events, direct messaging, and university-focused opportunities to encourage collaboration throughout the academic year.

---

# Why JustAsk?

College students often have valuable skills, free time, or small tasks they need help with, but there isn't a dedicated platform built specifically for them.

JustAsk aims to become that platform.

Instead of asking random people on WhatsApp groups or Discord servers, students can post requests, find opportunities, negotiate, collaborate, and build meaningful connections.

---

# Core Categories

## 🎓 Skill Exchange

A place where students exchange skills instead of money.

Examples:

- I'll teach you Guitar if you teach me Singing.
- Teach me Flutter, and I'll help you with React.
- I'll teach Calculus in exchange for learning UI/UX Design.

The goal is to encourage collaborative learning between students.

---

## 💼 Work & Earn

Students can post small jobs and allow others to bid for them.

Example:

> Need my assignment handwritten.
>
> Budget: ₹200

Other students can respond:

- I'll do it for ₹180.
- I'll do it for ₹150.
- I'll do it for ₹100.

The poster chooses the most suitable offer and both parties continue the conversation through Direct Messages.

Possible use cases include:

- Assignment writing
- Poster design
- Presentation creation
- Video editing
- Coding help
- Resume reviews
- Notes preparation
- Tutoring

---

## 🤝 Volunteer

Not every request needs money.

Students who simply want to help can volunteer their time or skills.

Examples:

- I'll mentor first-year students.
- Free resume review.
- Helping juniors prepare for interviews.
- Teaching programming basics.
- Helping organize college events.

This category encourages community building and peer support.

---

# Seasonal Events

JustAsk can adapt throughout the academic year by introducing temporary community sections.

Examples include:

- Smart India Hackathon Team Formation
- Hackathon Partner Finder
- Internship Season
- Placement Preparation
- Exam Discussion Hubs
- Fresher Guidance
- College Fest Volunteers

These sections appear only during relevant periods, making the platform more useful throughout the year.

---

# Direct Messaging

Once two students agree on a deal or collaboration, they can continue the conversation through private Direct Messages.

DMs can be used to:

- Discuss project details
- Negotiate pricing
- Share resources
- Coordinate meetings
- Continue collaborations
